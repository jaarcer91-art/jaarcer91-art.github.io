Founder, Miami

Jose Arce

Building the private home for the next generation of wealth.

Jose Arce is a two-time founder in Miami. He is building Olie, a private home for the next generation of wealth: three apps behind one front door. Olie Vault to protect what you built, Olie Orbit to pay your friends and Olie Business to pay the world.

Before Olie, he spent four years at OKX and Okcoin designing how millions of people buy, earn and pay with digital dollars. At OKX he led the Earn team and its complete redesign, created Lira Boost for Turkey, designed Rewards for OKX Pay and launched OKX Lite, the first app made for everyday buyers.

His first company, Mone, let merchants across Latin America get paid by QR code, online and in person. He designed every screen of it himself, and Mone was acquired. Earlier, he redesigned the platforms every medical student in the United States passes through at the AAMC.
