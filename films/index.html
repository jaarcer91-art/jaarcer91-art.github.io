How they are made

A small studio, run like a product team

The same process for every film, so a 64 second film takes days, not months.

  1. 1

    Brief and reference

    One sentence on what the film must make someone feel, and the reference films for rhythm and light. References guide the idea, never the look.

  2. 2

    Options before credits

    Three directions on one page with stills and a storyboard, chosen before a single generation is paid for.

  3. 3

    One draft per part

    Each part generated as a low resolution draft and approved before the next. Most parts land on the first or second take because the prompt is a full shot list.

  4. 4

    One face, every scene

    A single reference frame of the lead keeps him the same person across a study, a yacht and a pool.

  5. 5

    The real product, on the glass

    The app is never mocked up. A real recording of Olie plays on the phone inside the scene.

  6. 6

    Voice, score and captions

    Narration in English or Spanish, one music track that starts when the product appears, and captions burned in for silent feeds.
