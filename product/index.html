08How Jose works

Bring the hard problem. He makes it work, from the first line to the store.

  1. 01

    Bring the hard problem

    A blank page, or a product that works but does not convince yet.

  2. 02

    He builds it with your team, end to end

    Research, the flows, the brand and every screen, then iOS, Android and web, from the ticket to production.

  3. 03

    It ships, and it holds up

    Every size, colour, timing and vibration is specified and measured, so the product holds together screen after screen.
