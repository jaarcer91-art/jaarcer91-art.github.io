Olie

People in emerging markets save in dollars. Holding them takes a bank they must trust, a US visa, or cash at home. Olie is the company I built to remove all three: first as one dollar app, then as three products.

2024 to 2026
Research

People save in dollars. Keeping them safe is the problem.

At OKX I led LATAM research and spent two months in Brazil, talking to users and to people who had never touched crypto. What I found was bigger than one app. These are the problems Olie, the company, was built around.

The local currency moves with politics

They earn in local currency and save in dollars. In August 2019 the Argentine peso lost 15 to 23% of its value in one day, after a primary election.

Al Jazeera, AFP and S&P, August 12, 2019.

Savings get frozen, again and again

Banks, governments and crypto platforms have locked people out of their money in six different years.

Yale Journal of Financial Crises. US bankruptcy filings, 2022. Federal Reserve review, April 2023. FGC and Reuters, 2026. Banco Central del Paraguay, November 2026.

Problem statement

People in LATAM need to hold and move dollars without depending on a local bank, a visa or a trading app.

Everyday dollars, with friends. Get paid from abroad and pay a friend back in seconds.

Wealth kept for the long run, at an address the family can check themselves.

Companies paying the world. Accounts in dollars and euros, suppliers paid the same day.

2025
The bet

A dollar app where you can check your own money.

An app that looks like a bank and holds real dollars: USDC, the most regulated digital dollar, moved in and out on banking rails. One decision defined it.

One shared ledger, or an address for every person

A ledger is quick to build through a provider. An address per person takes months, and anyone can check it.

Four walls before the first user

Money is regulated country by country. Each of these had to be solved before anyone could sign up.

Licences

Found the one LATAM market where a legal opinion confirmed no licence was needed, and launched there.

Identity

Identity checks at sign up, AML and OFAC screening on every user.

Bank rails

Signed a licensed US partner. Accounts in the user’s own name, by ACH, wire and SEPA.

Regulators

Met regulators and lined up local partners in Argentina and Peru.

Decision

We built the hard one. A bank can never show you your own money, because it lends it out.
